We completed the same expanded reconciliation for the Humana FFM population against the 834 inbound automation data, comparing enrollee and policy identifiers across the available inbound history.

The analysis identified 28,218 exact Enrollee ID + Policy ID pairs present on both sides. These exact pairs represent 25,796 distinct enrollees and 28,183 distinct policies. The pair count is higher because an enrollee may be associated with multiple policies, and a policy may contain multiple household members.

For the remaining population, 29,997 pairs (31.45%) currently have no inbound enrollee evidence, while 12,136 pairs (12.72%) have the enrollee present inbound but under a different lifecycle. We also identified 9,031 cross-issuer transitions (9.47%), where the same enrollee was found under another issuer lifecycle.

An additional 15,641 pairs (16.40%) were associated with the same issuer but a different policy; however, only 355 pairs (0.37%) met the broader potential policy-identifier mismatch criteria, and only 116 pairs (0.12%) met the stricter same-transaction/different-policy criteria. This again indicates that policy ID differences alone do not appear to explain the majority of the reconciliation gap.

We are continuing to validate the no-inbound and lifecycle categories against transaction history and UI evidence before finalizing the root-cause conclusions.
